faq:
  - question: What is Agentic AI?
    answer: An AI Agent is an AI system built on large language models (LLMs). In this architecture, the LLM acts as the brain, responsible for language understanding and reasoning, while the agent functions as the body, executing tasks and interacting with tools and systems. Agentic AI extends this model into a fully autonomous digital agent. It can interpret goals, break them into structured steps, select appropriate tools, plan workflows, and execute tasks end-to-end with minimal human intervention.
  - question: Do I need a unified AI Agent management platform?
    answer: "Enterprises often deploy multiple AI Agents across different functions. Without centralized governance, this can lead to operational inefficiencies, security risks, and compliance challenges. A unified Agent platform ensures AI operates in a controlled and transparent environment while delivering business value. Security & Governance: Provides full lifecycle management for AI models and agents, consolidates shadow AI, and enforces identity and access controls to prevent unauthorized access and abnormal behavior. Data Interoperability: Standardizes integration across systems to eliminate data silos and enable cross-team collaboration and efficient workflows. Cost Transparency: Monitors token consumption in real time, sets usage limits, and dynamically allocates models and agents based on task complexity to optimize resource usage."
  - question: What is the difference between OrientAI and an API management platform?
    answer: OrientAI, as an AI Agent management platform (Agentic AI platform), is essentially a hub that integrates various AI APIs, allowing AI to access enterprise data and operate systems within a controllable range.
  - question: Will my data be used to train third-party models?

  - question: What are the common challenges when adopting AI Agents?
    answer: "As Agentic AI becomes more widely adopted, increasing autonomy also introduces new governance complexity. Common challenges include: Fragmented workflows: Lack of unified orchestration and monitoring across systems hinders coordination between agents. Security and compliance risks: Insufficient access control and poor data governance increases the risk of data exposure. Increased costs: Invisibility of token consumption makes costs unpredictable. Technical constraints: Adoption stalls due to integration issues and misaligned business logics."
