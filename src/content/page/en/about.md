  - heading: Milestones
    body: We Inspire Innovation to Help Businesses Thrive in the Digital Era.
    items:
      - title: '2005'
        body: Founded in 2005, TPIsoftware (formerly named ThinkPower) built custom enterprise digital systems with its strong technology capabilities. With the business philosophy of delivering exceptional services, Ben Yao, the founder of TPIsoftware, seeks to become a lifetime partner to customers and grow together. Ben firmly believes Taiwan’s software talents can compete with global tech giants, and aspires to take these top talents to the world stage.
      - title: 2006 - 2010
        body: TPIsoftware provided software quality management and consulting services to TSMC and Ericsson with its extensive experience and expertise. It entered the financial sector by building the CRM system for Hua Nan Bank in 2008, which was awarded Spectacular Corporate Achievement 2008.
      - title: 2011 - 2015
        body: In 2011, it further developed its proprietary software product TP Recommendation System (TPRS). TPIsoftware is trusted by iconic BFSI clients such as Cathay Financial Holdings, Taishin bank, Prudential plc, ANZ Bank, BNP Paribas Cardif and many more for its dedication to digital transformation.
      - title: 2016 - 2019
        body: TPIsoftware became a leading FinTech solution provider in Taiwan with over 400 successful projects. It established its Singapore and Vietnam subsidiaries in 2017 and 2019 respectively to expand its overseas footprint. Furthermore, it rolled out its in-house developed software products DigiLism and SysTalk.ai, with the AI Chatbot awarded Cool Vendor 2018 in APAC by Gartner.
      - title: 2020 - 2022
        body: Following its rebranding in 2020, TPIsoftware has become the key player in Taiwan’s software industry with 630 employees worldwide. It has been selected as a top FT 500 Asia-Pacific High-Growth Company of 2022, and globally recognized by Taiwan Excellence Awards 2023, Taiwan SMEs Innovation Award 2022, Future Commerce Awards 2022, CX Asia Excellence Awards 2021, Digital Financial Service Award 2021 and more.
