## 1.    What is AI Knowledge Management? How is it Different From an AI Assistant?

**Knowledge management (KM) powered by artificial intelligence** has increasingly gained popularity among companies seeking to use AI tools to yield desired outcomes with enhanced efficiency.

As the AI hype spirals, tech vendors providing AI-powered KM solutions tend to advertise their products as **AI assistants for enterprises.** This, of course, leads to misaligned expectations and poor experiences. Customers expect an AI assistant but what they get is a knowledge management tool.

AI KM tools are **structured repositories equipped with generative AI** (GenAI) used to retrieve, summarize and search for information based on keywords. Managing corporate knowledge with this approach provides greater accessibility to the information needed to perform tasks. AI assistants, on the other hand, are AI-powered applications that understand natural language commands and provide users with the most relevant information or assistance.

AI KM integrates AI technologies to streamline knowledge management processes, while AI assistants focus on automating tasks that used to be done by humans. When comparing AI KM and AI assistants, it is important to look at **how they are built, used and deployed**.

## 2.    AI KM and AI Assistants Vary in How They Are Built, Used and Deployed

### The way they are built is different.

-   **AI Knowledge Management** makes knowledge accessible and searchable with AI enhancement.

-   **AI Assistants** leverage AI reasoning and natural language understanding (NLU) to provide responses to queries, trigger actions and support decision-making.

### How they are used is different.

-   To get the most out of **AI knowledge management**, users need to know how to use tags, filters or queries to search for information they need.

-   Users interact with **AI assistants** in a conversational way using human language.

### The scope of deployment is different.

|  | **AI Knowledge Management** | **AI Assistants** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Functionalities** | Optimizes search, categorization and document summarization | Q&A, decision support, automated responses, multi-role interaction |
| **User Experience** | Smarter search engine used within an organization | Feels like working with a human assistant or colleague |
| **Application Scenarios** | Internal use for information retrieval | Broad use cases across customer service, HR, IT support, sales training, etc. |

## **3.**   **What Are the Risks of AI Knowledge Management**

Implementing an AI knowledge management platform has several risks:

-   **Misaligned expectations:** Users end up searching for information in a manual way as the AI KM does not align with their needs

-   **Limited results:** The AI KM does not integrate data, provide sufficient guidance or handle specific tasks, resulting in low adoption rate.

-   **Increased costs:** Additional resources are required to expand usage into other use cases to support broader applications across teams and functions.

Instead, deploying an AI assistant powered by generative AI helps:

-   Convert documents, conversations and process manuals into machine-readable text, which can then be utilized by AI to generate outputs.

-   Easily create conversational AI assistants that support multiple departments such as HR, customer service and IT.

-   Provide personalized responses and recommendations based on roles and permissions.

-   Integrate agents to proactively execute tasks or respond flexibly.

## **4.**    **See the Differences in These Use Cases**

### HR: Handling Policy Queries

-   **AI Knowledge Management Platform:** Keywords like *taking leave* or *compensatory time off* are used to find relevant information.

-    **AI Assistants:** Responses are provided to answer specific questions. For example, when someone asks the AI assistant if they are allowed to take compensatory leave in certain cases, it identifies the user intent and guides them on what to do next.

### Customer Service: Handling Returns and Product Replacement

-   **AI Knowledge Management Platform:** Human involvement is required to determine what actions should be taken to address customer requests. The process has to be guided by human representatives.

-    **AI Assistants:** Steps are recommended based on the context of customer requests. Replies to customer inquiries can be drafted as needed.

### MIS: Resolving Common IT Issues

-   **AI Knowledge Management Platform:** Keywords like *VPN* or *installation* are used to find manual guides for instructions.

-   **AI Assistants:** When someone asks the AI assistant how they can connect to the company’s intranet from home, the AI helps them address the technical issue by giving instructions based on what device they use.
