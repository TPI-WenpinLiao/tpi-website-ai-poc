## What Is AI-Powered Speech Analytics?

AI-powered speech analytics uses speech recognition and artificial intelligence to analyze spoken conversations and extract meaningful information from customer interactions.

In contact centers, speech analytics helps organizations examine call content, understand customer concerns, evaluate service quality and identify communication patterns that may require attention.

### How Does AI Speech Analytics Work?

A typical speech analytics workflow begins with recorded conversations. Speech recognition converts spoken content into text, while AI analyzes the resulting conversation to identify relevant information.

SysTalk.Audit combines speech transcription with contextual analysis to support customer service quality assessment, sentiment analysis and compliance-related reviews.

-   **Speech Transcription:** Convert recorded conversations into text for analysis and review.

-   **Contextual Understanding:** Interpret conversational meaning rather than isolated keywords.

-   **Quality Assessment:** Evaluate interactions against defined quality expectations.

-   **Actionable Reporting:** Organize findings to support quality assurance and follow-up.

### Speech Analytics vs. Traditional Keyword-Based Monitoring

Traditional keyword-based monitoring identifies predefined words or phrases. However, the same expression may have different meanings depending on the surrounding conversation.

For example, a customer may use positive words sarcastically when describing an unsatisfactory experience. A keyword-only approach may fail to recognize the underlying negative sentiment.

By considering conversational context, SysTalk.Audit helps quality assurance teams interpret customer interactions with greater contextual understanding.
